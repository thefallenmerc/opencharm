# Bundled music — licenses

All five tracks below are from **FreePD.com**, a collection of Kevin MacLeod
compositions that FreePD explicitly dedicated to the **public domain** under
**CC0 1.0 Universal** (`https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode`)
— no attribution required, free for any use including commercial.

FreePD.com itself went offline in 2025 ("The Music Has Moved On" — site closure
notice, permanently retired after 17 years). The original files are still
reachable through the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine mirror of the site, and
that's where these copies were downloaded from (URLs below). Each file was
re-encoded from the archived source to 96 kbps MP3 (stereo, 44.1 kHz) to keep
the bundle small; content/composition is unchanged.
